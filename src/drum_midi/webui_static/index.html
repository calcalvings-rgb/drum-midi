Space plays/pauses WAV; click the ruler to seek. Click to draw; drag to move; drag a note edge to resize. Shift + drag selects an area. Right-click deletes. Ctrl A selects all; Ctrl B duplicates; Ctrl Z undoes; Ctrl Y or Ctrl Shift Z redoes. The lower lane edits velocity.

Snap moves in grid-sized steps and preserves an imported hit's offset. Quantize aligns selected hits to the grid. Edits stay local until you export. MIDI input uses GM pitches. Downloads use the selected output kit and BPM on drum channel 10. Note events only; variable tempo/meter maps are not supported.
